About / The Collection

Every piece passes
through one set of hands.

Faxxelange is a small collection of fine gold and natural gemstone jewelry, chosen piece by piece rather than bought by the case. Rings, chains, bangles, earrings, pendants set with emerald, citrine, tourmaline, and diamond, each one is inspected under a loupe, weighed, and given its own lot number before it goes up for sale.

The name on this site is ours. The eye is ours. Every listing here was written after the piece was in hand, measured and checked, not copied from a spec sheet.

The Standards

What we check before a lot is listed.

Six things get looked at on every piece, gold or silver, gemstone or plain metal, before it's cleared to carry a Faxxelange number.

01 Metal

Marked and tested for karat weight. 10K, 14K, and 18K gold only, plus fine silver where the listing says so.

02 Stones

Natural gemstones and diamonds are named as such in the listing. Nothing is called natural unless we've confirmed it ourselves.

03 Condition

Clasps, hinges, and prongs are checked by hand. Wear that comes with age is noted plainly, not hidden behind a good photo angle.

04 Weight & Size

Every piece is weighed and measured so the listing reflects the object in hand, chain length, ring size, bangle diameter, and all.

05 Origin

Where a piece carries a maker's mark or a known history, such as our Michael Anthony charm pieces, we say so in the listing.

06 Description

Each lot is photographed and written on its own. No stock photos, no description borrowed from a similar piece.

Process

How a piece gets from a table to a lot number.

01

Sourced

Pieces come from estate lots, trade partners, and private sellers, chosen one at a time rather than bought in bulk.

02

Examined

Each item is cleaned, tested, and checked against what it's represented to be before it earns a place in the collection.

03

Catalogued

Weight, karat, stone type, and measurements are recorded and written into the listing in plain language.

04

Offered

The piece is photographed on its own and listed once, under its own number, here on our shop.
